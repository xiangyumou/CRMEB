# CR-34-k2 — the storefront serves a draft presale activity by id

**Stream:** K2 (hardening second pass) **Status:** OPEN — for the orchestrator (D2's service; D2 merged)
**Found by:** `next/e2e/admin/specs/presale.spec.ts`, "a draft is not on the storefront, not even by id" (`test.fail`)
**Files:** `next/packages/core/src/presale/presale.service.ts` (`detail`),
`next/packages/core/src/presale/presale.repo.ts` (`findActivity`)

## What

`GET /api/v1/presale/activities` lists only activities that are `active` and
inside their window. `GET /api/v1/presale/activities/:id` calls
`repo.findActivity(id)`, which has no status filter, so it answers **200** for a
`draft` (and a `paused`) activity: title, intro, images, presale price, every
enabled 规格 with its price and stock. `canBuy` is `false`, so nothing can be
bought — this is a disclosure, not a sale.

Two sentences in the product promise otherwise: the form's help text on 状态
(「草稿不会出现在前台」) and the not-found error's own wording
(`PRESALE_ACTIVITY_NOT_FOUND`: 「预售活动不存在或已下架」). Ids are sequential,
so an unreleased campaign's price is one increment away. The catalogue already
behaves the right way: a draft product is 404 on the storefront
(`product.spec.ts`, "a draft product is not on the storefront").

## Asked for

In `detail`, answer `PRESALE_ACTIVITY_NOT_FOUND` unless the activity is
`active` or `ended` (an ended campaign's page is still linked from orders, and
`canBuy` already says no). A `paused` campaign is the operator's "stop selling
now"; whether its page stays readable is D2's call — the spec accepts either a
404 or `canBuy: false` for it.
