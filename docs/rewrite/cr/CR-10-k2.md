## What

`POST /api/v1/staff/coupon-grants` is gated on the roster (a non-staff shopper
gets 403 — `coupons.staff.int.test.ts::POST /api/v1/staff/coupon-grants > 403s for a signed-in shopper who is not on the roster`).
Behind it, `staffGrant` is `adminGrant` with one user id, and `adminGrant`
takes any live template id. So a 店员 — a storefront account with a roster
row, not a console user — can:

- grant a `draft` or `disabled` template (the staff coupon list offers only
  `active` ones, but the grant does not check), e.g. a ¥50 coupon marketing has
  not released;
- grant it to their own `userId` or an accomplice's, up to `perUserLimit`;
- and none of it leaves an audit row (CR-13-k2).

## Asked for

In `staffGrant` (not `adminGrant`, whose console caller may legitimately grant
a draft to a test account):

- refuse a template whose status is not `active` (`COUPON_TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND`,
  the same answer the list would give);
- refuse `body.userId === ctx.actor.id` with a domain error;
- flip both `it.fails`.

Whether a per-staff daily cap is wanted is a product question; record it either
way.
