**Pinned by:** `next/packages/core/src/coupon/coupon.staff-grant.int.test.ts::K-SEC-B3 — what a 店员 can grant > refuses a template marketing has not released, or has withdrawn`, `> refuses to grant to the 店员’s own account` (both `it.fails`)

## What

`POST /api/v1/staff/coupon-grants` is gated on the roster (a non-staff shopper
gets 403 — `coupons.staff.int.test.ts::POST /api/v1/staff/coupon-grants > 403s for a signed-in shopper who is not on the roster`).
Behind it, `staffGrant` is `adminGrant` with one user id, and `adminGrant`
takes any live template id. So a 店员 — a storefront account with a roster
row, not a console user — can:

- grant a `draft` or `disabled` template (the staff coupon list offers only
  `active` ones, but the grant does not check), e.g. a ¥50 coupon marketing has
  not released;
- grant it to their own `userId` or an accomplice's, up to `perUserLimit`;
- and none of it leaves an audit row (CR-13-k2).

## Asked for

In `staffGrant` (not `adminGrant`, whose console caller may legitimately grant
a draft to a test account):

- refuse a template whose status is not `active` (`COUPON_TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND`,
  the same answer the list would give);
- refuse `body.userId === ctx.actor.id` with a domain error;
- flip both `it.fails`.

Whether a per-staff daily cap is wanted is a product question; record it either
way.

## Until then

Review `user_coupons` rows with `source_kind = 'admin_grant'` whose user is on
the staff roster.

## Resolution (R4, wave 6)

- `coupon.service.ts`: `adminGrant` and `staffGrant` share a private
  `grant(ctx, templateId, userIds, { activeOnly })`. `staffGrant` fails closed
  unless the actor is `staff`, refuses `userId === actor.id` with the new
  `COUPON_GRANT_SELF` (422, `不能给自己发放优惠券`, added to
  `contracts/coupon/errors.ts` and the route's `errors`), and passes
  `activeOnly: true` — the status is read inside the grant's transaction and a
  `draft` / `disabled` template answers `COUPON_TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND`.
  `adminGrant` is unchanged in behaviour (the console may grant a draft).
- Tests: both `it.fails` in `coupon.staff-grant.int.test.ts` flipped; added
  "answers with the code, and leaves the web console free to grant a draft"
  there and "404s a draft template and 422s a grant to the 店员’s own account,
  touching nothing" in `apps/web/app/api/v1/coupons.staff.int.test.ts`.
- **Product question (not built):** a per-staff daily cap on grants. The
  audit rows CR-13-k2 now writes for the staff surface
  (`routeId = 'coupon.staffGrant'`, `actor_kind = 'staff'`, `user_id`) are the
  data a cap — or a review of one — would be read from.
- Self-grant is the only collusion check: granting to an accomplice's account
  is indistinguishable from a real customer and is left to that review.
